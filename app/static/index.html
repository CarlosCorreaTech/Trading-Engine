Trading Engine

 

What the engine noticed

Every detected change is labelled with what kind of thing moved. Only one of the four kinds is actionable as stated, and the classification is what routes a measurement fault to engineering instead of to marketing.

What is wrong with the data

These checks run as part of the build. Errors stop it. Known issues are failures that were investigated and are expected to fail permanently; they do not stop the build, they change what the affected metrics may be used for. The score each metric family earns here becomes a ceiling on the confidence of any recommendation built on it.

Running the pipeline

Detecting signals, scoring recommendations, simulating 10,000 outcomes each, then gating.
